# Submission checklist

The following are team handoff items. Check the current organizer instructions before treating any draft as a submission.

## Per member

- [ ] Boss reviews authorship, confirms first-person statements, publishes the distinct Boss article and records the public URL. [Boss article](boss-article.md)
- [ ] L reviews authorship, confirms first-person statements, publishes the distinct L article and records the public URL. [L article](l-article.md)
- [ ] Each member posts their own LinkedIn draft and adds the comment with their published article URL and Hindsight repository link. [Boss LinkedIn](boss-linkedin.md) · [L LinkedIn](l-linkedin.md)
- [ ] Each member checks public visibility and provides their article and social URLs in the organizer’s required fields.

## Team

- [ ] Rehearse with a fresh isolated fictional database, verify both demo roles and keep a local ledger-only fallback.
- [ ] Rehearse live Hindsight retain, recall and reflect with valid configuration. Show Memory OFF versus ON and actual evidence.
- [ ] Record one public team video, **2–5 minutes**, **1080p**, targeting the supplied 3–4 minute script. [Video script](video-script.md) · [YouTube draft](youtube.md)
- [ ] Review the final video for factual accuracy, legibility, captions and absence of credentials, runtime data and private identifiers.
- [ ] Have the authorized uploader publish on YouTube, verify visibility while signed out and capture the public URL.
- [ ] Choose the article for the Reddit link post, replace its placeholder and verify the community rules before posting. [Reddit draft](reddit.md)
- [ ] Confirm the organizer’s Band AI Desktop requirement and what evidence they accept. Have a team member complete and document actual use if required. This release preparation did not install or use Band.
- [ ] Review source, tests and secret exclusions. Obtain explicit approval before publishing changes to the public GitHub repository. Verify the public revision contains the intended files.
- [ ] Prepare the live demo and explain the ledger authority, eligibility rules, bank scope, consent and provider failure handling.
- [ ] Cross-check names, team details and public URLs against the current organizer forms. Have the authorized submitter complete the forms and save confirmation.
